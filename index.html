Frequently asked questions

What can I do with AI Photo Editor & Enhancer?

You can retouch portraits, enhance photo quality, remove unwanted objects, swap faces, remove or change backgrounds, apply filters and transform your photos into AI artwork — all in one app.

How do I remove unwanted objects from a photo?

Open the object removal tool, adjust the brush size, paint over the area you want to erase, and the AI will clean it up to create a neater-looking image.

Can I compare my original photo with the enhanced result?

Yes. After enhancing, you can compare the original picture with the processed result side by side before saving.

Which devices are supported?
